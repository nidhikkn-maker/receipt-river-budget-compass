LEDGERLINE v6.1 — LIVE MARKET DATA & DYNAMIC NET WORTH

Integration with Demat Controls and Ledgerline v6.0

1. Objective

Implement automated market-price retrieval, portfolio valuation and dynamic net-worth calculations.

The module must use existing Demat holdings, investment records, bank balances, assets and liabilities.

Do not create duplicate investment holdings or financial transactions.

2. Market-data integration

Support authorised providers for:

* NSE-listed equities.
* BSE-listed equities.
* Listed ETFs.
* Listed bonds and other supported securities.
* Mutual fund NAVs.
* Relevant currencies.
* Other supported market instruments.

Provider integrations must comply with their access, licensing, caching and redistribution conditions.

Do not use unauthorised scraping or circumvent website restrictions.

3. Security identification

Match investments using:

* ISIN.
* Exchange.
* Symbol.
* Instrument type.
* Relevant fund identifiers.

Do not rely solely on security names.

4. Automatic valuation

Calculate:

Holding Value = Verified Quantity × Applicable Market Price

Portfolio Value = Sum of Recognised Holding Values + Applicable Investment-Account Cash

Use appropriate instrument-specific valuation rules.

Do not double-count investment cash or securities appearing in multiple account statements.

5. Market status

For every instrument, display:

* Exchange.
* Trading status.
* Last available price.
* Price date and time.
* Data provider.
* Live/delayed/end-of-day status.
* Last successful refresh.
* Staleness indicator.

Do not label delayed or previous-close data as live.

6. Automatic updates

During market hours, refresh eligible securities according to the authorised feed’s capabilities and permitted frequency.

Outside market hours, retain the latest valid closing or available price with its timestamp.

If a market-data request fails, preserve the previous valid value and mark it stale.

Do not substitute zero for missing prices.

7. Mutual fund NAV

Use an authorised, reliable NAV source.

Recognise that ordinary mutual fund NAVs are generally published for a valuation day rather than changing continuously like exchange-traded shares.

Refresh NAVs when newly published.

Show the NAV date.

8. Gold

Maintain separate valuations for:

* Gold ETFs.
* Sovereign Gold Bonds.
* Physical gold.

Gold ETFs may use appropriate exchange-traded prices.

Physical gold requires a configured valuation method, purity and weight.

Do not automatically treat an exchange-traded gold security’s price as the value of physical gold.

9. Dynamic net worth

Recalculate net worth using:

* Latest reliable bank balances.
* Current investment valuations.
* Other recognised assets.
* Outstanding liabilities.
* Correct legal ownership.

Net Worth = Recognised Assets − Recognised Liabilities

Distinguish market-valued assets from bank-verified cash and manually valued assets.

10. Net-worth freshness

Display a valuation timestamp and data-quality summary.

For example:

* Listed investments: Market price as of current trading session.
* Mutual funds: Latest published NAV.
* Bank accounts: Latest reconciled statement or authorised bank feed.
* Property: Latest dated valuation.
* Loans: Latest verified outstanding balance.

Do not label overall net worth as fully live when important components are not live.

11. Home integration

Add a dynamic Net Worth summary to the Ledgerline Home page.

Show:

* Net worth.
* Investment portfolio value.
* Investment movement.
* Liquid balances.
* Liabilities.
* Last update.
* Data freshness.

Provide a tap-through to the full Financial Position and Investments modules.

12. Historical valuations

Maintain dated valuation snapshots for:

* Portfolio history.
* Net-worth history.
* Investment performance.
* Asset allocation changes.

Snapshots must not create new ledger transactions.

13. Alerts

Support:

* Significant portfolio changes.
* Missing market prices.
* Stale NAVs.
* Corporate actions requiring reconciliation.
* Holding quantity discrepancies.
* Unusual valuation differences.

Alerts must be configurable.

14. Testing

Verify:

1. Correct ISIN-to-security matching.
2. Different exchanges and instrument types.
3. Real-time versus delayed price labels.
4. Market-open and market-closed behaviour.
5. Latest NAV updates.
6. Market-data feed failures.
7. Stale valuations.
8. Duplicate holding prevention.
9. Brokerage cash reconciliation.
10. Portfolio valuation accuracy.
11. Ownership-aware net worth.
12. No duplicate ledger postings.
13. iPhone/iPad/desktop responsiveness.

15. Restrictions

Preserve existing Demat, investment and financial records.

Do not invent current prices.

Do not claim market data is real-time without an authorised real-time source.

Do not alter historical purchase costs from market-price updates.

Do not execute trades.

Do not modify shared financial data or database schemas without approval.

Do not deploy to production without explicit approval.

Implement in isolation and submit market-data source verification, valuation calculations, screenshots and test evidence for approval.
