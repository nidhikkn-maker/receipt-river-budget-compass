# Books, tax and compliance rules
- Company member originals use exact owner/company UTF-8 hex paths through company-upload.ts; staged company-bound-uploads.sql tests owner-verified grants, uploader-bound cleanup and metadata path binding. Do not install on Live without compatible deployment and access-change approval.
- Broker cash checks compare original currency/date/amount/account and narration with unique matches only; they never post missing movements or value securities from a combined cash account. Investment originals use owner-content identities and evidence locks; interrupted account ids are retained during review.
- Company/person profiles (src/lib/entities.ts) live in owner preferences.entities; PAN type letter and GSTIN embedded PAN/mod-36 check are validated before save, and financial-year status is counted from saved records only, never marked complete automatically.
- Supplier/missing-invoice checks (src/lib/gst-recon.ts) are read-only over business-scope spending; a bill counts if the entry has a receipt or a DocuRepo document linked to it, and GSTIN is read only from linked documents.
- Property/fixed assets (src/lib/assets.ts) live in owner preferences.assets; book SLM/WDV is day-prorated, tax WDV uses block rates with the under-180-day half-rate rule, land never depreciates, and rent checks only read income entries naming the property.
- Income tax/GST working (src/lib/tax-summary.ts) is a read-only per-FY draft from ledger entries and assets; GST input counts only tax printed on business bills, never claimable credit.
- GSTR-2B matching (gstr2b.ts) reads portal JSON on-device only, one-to-one by supplier name word, ±7 days, ₹1/5%; audit-dashboard.ts only aggregates existing read-only rules, so findings clear when records are fixed.
- v6 books (src/lib/books/) are registers around the one ledger, stored in owner preferences.books: Statement Desk routes non-bank documents through DocumentIntake (AI read → pure propose* → revision-checked atomic intake commit; failed runs retain the original for recovery); closed periods are enforced by the guard_closed_period trigger on expenses; regulatory rules activate only after approval with dated versions; market prices keep their real freshness label and are never invented or zeroed.
- Approved asset depreciation posts once per asset-month (stable UUID from asset+month) as a non-cash adjustment on the asset's own other_asset/investment account, never a bank account; closed periods and unlinked assets are refused (books/assets-ledger.ts).
- NSE/BSE end-of-day closes come from official bhavcopy files saved by a cron route into shared market_quotes; demat holdings are the canonical copy of a security, so a manual holding with the same ISIN is never added to net worth twice.
- Official rule pages are watched weekly (books/rule-watch.ts): headline fingerprints record detected changes only; rules change solely through owner-approved proposals.
- Filing obligations, ITC review, tax credits and rule impact are pure rules in books/compliance.ts; due dates come from approved `due.<FORM>[.<period>]` register rules over standard defaults, so extensions live in one versioned place and nothing is filed automatically.
- Opening balances (books/fy.ts openingPlan + OpeningBalances.tsx) post dated 1 April `adjustment` entries per linked account via the atomic post_opening_balances RPC with stable entry ids; unbalanced/unlinked lines need a written exception, and undo_opening_balances removes only unedited entries — account opening_balance fields are never rewritten.
- Accountant ITR handoff (books/itr-handoff.ts, ItrHandoff.tsx) is a browser-built ZIP frozen as an evidence-locked DocuRepo document and tracked in books.itrHandoffs; handoffGaps lists only what records cannot supply and never fills a figure.
- Full tax liability (books/tax-full.ts) is pure per-FY rules over taxcomp output; company tax needs a chosen profile option or shows nothing. GST cash uses gstSetOff legal order. Frozen years: database preference triggers protect GST invoices, overlapping lease and tax-profile facts, asset financial facts, period definitions and append-only period history; physical checks remain editable.
- Mutual fund statements (books/fund-statement.ts) propose holdings from printed units/NAV only, matched to existing holdings by ISIN, then scheme code, then name, so they update rather than duplicate; NAV is saved as a "statement" price.
- Company↔account links (books/entity-accounts.ts) live in books.entityAccounts: many accounts per company, one company per account; companies can be created with only a name (no field is mandatory, PAN/GSTIN issues are warnings), and linking never changes entries.
- Entries carry entity_id/entity_source on expenses: "manual" keeps a hand-picked company, "account" follows books.entityAccounts via effectiveEntity(); personal tax adds business income only per books/from-entities.ts (proprietor profit; partner remuneration/interest within s.40(b), share exempt s.10(2A); company salary/dividend/deemed dividend s.2(22)(e) — never company profit).
- Company tax facts per FY (books/entity-tax-profile.ts, books.entityTaxProfiles) are optional owner entries; TaxCentre feeds them into from-entities (ownership %, capital, accumulated profits, book-profit override) and blanks stay unknown/flagged. The tax workpaper (books/tax-workpaper.ts + TaxWorkpaper.tsx) only regroups existing computations, source entries and assumptions; exports are evidence-locked in DocuRepo.
- Monthly depreciation is prepared (never posted) by the monthly-depreciation cron via scheduledRun with stable asset-month ids and an updated_at revision check.
- Asset physical checks resolve exact owner-visible QR/barcode tags through asset-verification.ts; lazy device-only decoding requires confirmation and saves the check/event together in preferences without financial changes.
- Accounting close/reopen uses change_accounting_period: owner-row serialization with ledger writes, canonical record snapshots, SHA-256 revisions and append-only owner-readable evidence; reopening retains prior evidence rather than editing it. Snapshots are saved-record evidence, never statutory certification.
- Document intake uses commit_document_intake_reviewed with owner-row revision guards, atomic evidence locking and request replay checks; all preference updates bump the common revision, so stale document saves cannot overwrite ordinary edits. Runs retain the exact approved operation/payload/revision before committing, so interrupted retries never regenerate identifiers; refresh/review checks for an already committed request before replacing it.
- Portfolio contributions/redemptions use investment-flows.ts over both investment and transfer movements crossing linked investment/deposit accounts; snapshots and alerts share signed flows, never changes in acquisition cost. Closing quotes retain the actual saved fetched_at and date-only price date, never invented refresh times.
- Source corrections use exact label mappings in source-corrections.ts to refresh typed proposals while preserving printed originals; unknown/ambiguous labels remain document details and never silently alter financial values.
- Standalone loan net-worth balances use loan-balances.ts dated owner-confirmed retained lender statements; missing/conflicting evidence is explicitly incomplete, never substituted from original or undated principal. Linked loans remain on the ledger path.
- Company access (books/entity-accounts.ts companyDocuments, CompanyAccess.tsx, migration 0075): view-only or edit grants per company via grant/revoke_entity_access (0076); members read only documents, files and transactions tagged with that entity_id, edit-grant members may update them but never retag to another company or personal; untagged documents stay owner-only; store loads only owner documents so shared ones never enter personal totals; members see shared companies through my_shared_companies (0077) and a separate SharedWithMe view with one-company CSV (shared-company.ts).
- Document intake commits are server-approved: commit_document_intake_reviewed (0079) refuses any save whose id/document/payload/lock/revision does not exactly match the approved attempt retained in the owner's document run; committed retries replay even after the run attempt is removed.
- Broker-cash overlap (recon.ts brokerCashOverlap) is a read-only audit finding when a broker_cash holding and a cash-like account share a significant name word; it never auto-excludes either side from net worth.
- Explicit broker-cash account links replace the manual cash amount with the cash-only ledger balance; names never decide exclusions. Bonds require retained original terms, explicit coupon period/day count and clean/dirty quote units; matured/unsupported periods stay incomplete, never auto-create redemption or interest entries.
- Broker cash currency is retained on the holding and converted with dated owner FX before valuation or ledger-gap comparison; linked ledger balances are already INR and are never converted again.
- Manual price overrides (books/price-overrides.ts, books.priceOverrides) replace any feed/close quote for their key while active and keep append-only set/changed/cleared history; required-report status lives in src/lib/report-status.ts and its test checks every referenced path exists.
- Register figure sources (books/evidence.ts) resolve document → DocuRepo link → linked entry, never inferred; GST notes/amendments flow through effectiveInvoices so every draft agrees, and closed-period invoices can only be corrected by notes.
- Intake acquisition documents link the existing payment explicitly; GST invoice identity includes direction, party and financial year to prevent unrelated overwrites, and ambiguous lease property names require selection.
- GST special treatment is explicit in gst-special.ts: exports stay out of domestic GSTR-1, service advances have reviewed invoice allocations, and reverse-charge tax is cash-only with credit gated on linked payment evidence; official-schema approval remains separate.
- Journals, financial asset events and regulatory rule transitions are guarded by the guard_journals_rules trigger (0083): posted journals are immutable, closed-period items frozen per company, approved rule versions are immutable history.
- Closed periods freeze derived figures too (0084 + period-figures.ts): the Audit close sends browser-computed version-1 figures (entry totals, GST heads, depreciation, closing balances) which change_accounting_period stores in the evidence with a SHA-256 figure revision and an owner-only account snapshot; PeriodEvidence re-derives them from the snapshot (reproducibility) and from current records (drift) — balances are drift-checked only, since the snapshot holds in-period entries.
- Derived figures keep dependency chains (derived-evidence.ts): GST payable per registration/month and posted depreciation list every input with its own resolved source; unsourced inputs are counted, never filled in. Advance adjustments may under-claim but never over-claim the pro-rata tax rate (advanceProblems) or the invoice they settle (advanceCrossProblems); gstr9Heads gives the per-head annual working. 0085 validates nested fyRecords schedules/audited heads/losses/filed returns/versions server-side.
- The staged preference-approval guard (.lovable/staged-sql/reviewed_preferences.sql) stays uninstalled until the owner approves publishing the compatible client; the compatibility matrix in preference-approval-sql.test.ts proves every existing write path (intake, period close, depreciation cron, finance_operation, restore) survives it.
- Atomic category merge and undo remain staged: exact before/after owner snapshots retain split/source fields and independent plans; reversal refuses any later edit and retains audit history. UI refuses split/trip references until compatible authority rollout is approved; phantom/concurrency compatibility remains unverified.
