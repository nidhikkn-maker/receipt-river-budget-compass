1. Decision and precedence

V3 remains the comprehensive product requirements master. This V3.1 addendum closes
documentation gaps, adds the 111-screen register, establishes technical handoff schemas, and makes
acceptance measurable. Nothing in this document deletes or weakens an approved V3 requirement.
Where requirements conflict, Claude must issue a Decision Needed record with both references and
stop that affected implementation. The hierarchy is: KK’s explicit later decision; applicable law and
authoritative forms (subject to review); V3.1 technical acceptance clarifications; V3 functional
requirements; approved Signature Flow screenshots for visual questions; existing production behavior
only where compatible and safe. Never silently choose between conflicts.

Authorization boundary: Claude may audit the connected GitHub repository and prepare changes in
an isolated branch or preview. No migration of live records, production data repair, external filing or
payment, transfer of ownership, deployment, DNS cutover, or publication without a separate explicit
authorization from KK. Keep ledgerline.biz operational and untouched.

Honesty rule: A requirement can be specified, coded, tested, deployed, or production-verified; these are
different states. Do not label any item completed merely because it appears in V3, a screenshot, a unit
test or a developer’s summary. All 50 decisions in V3 are marked Agreed; a proposal must never silently
replace one.

3. Product shape and boundary

Thapal is Home, Money, To-do, Records, with one central Add action. Reports is a direct rail
destination on wider layouts and accessible within two taps on iPhone. Calendar, Search, Notifications,
workspace selection, Help and Settings are globally accessible. All deeper functional modules live under
those entry points without separate, competing top-level navigation. Keep the current Statement Desk
ingestion engine as the sole authoritative financial-document intake. The apparent entry doors (Add,
email, file picker, scheduled upload link and module action) must converge on the same source, parsing,
deduplication and posting path.

All workspaces (personal, adult family members, minors, companies, trusts, HUFs and supported
organisations) must be legally and technically isolated. Combined group views show ownership and
explicit consolidation assumptions; they are not substitutes for separate entity books. An owner of one
entity does not automatically obtain another adult’s private data.

5. End-to-end accounting data contract

The canonical chain is source file → document version → extracted observations → economic event →
ownership and account mapping → approval policy → balanced posting → bank/card reconciliation →
derived module views → statutory drafts → reports and reminders → audit evidence.

Required immutable IDs: workspace; legal entity; person; account/card; source; document version;
parse attempt; financial event; accounting posting; posting line; match; expense; reimbursement; trip;
asset; property; investment lot; loan; tax working; calendar event; report; approval; audit event. Reuse
valid existing IDs and never overwrite history.

Database-level invariants: signed exact-decimal amounts; debits equal credits per posting, legal entity
and currency; unique idempotency key per economic event; no reuse of an invoice as full settlement for
two duplicate debits; partial receipts leave the balance due; card settlement does not create an expense;
inter-account transfer does not create income; same economic event appears once despite multiple
source files; reconciled amounts agree with independent statement balances; posted entries cannot be
silently edited; locked-period changes require authorised correcting entries; cross-workspace references
cannot bypass row-level permissions.

Posting policy: Unknown owner, account, currency, conflicting document values or unsupported tax
treatment creates a To-do exception, not a fabricated posting. Graduated automatic posting begins only
after the per-document and per-issuer evidence gate of V3 Decision 4 passes and KK’s policy permits
that event class. Payroll, property sale, taxes, unusual journals, locked periods and consequential
approvals retain their higher gates regardless of model confidence.

Reprocessing: Store parser/model version and extracted field provenance. New parsing of the same
source creates a new parse attempt and comparison, not a second posting. Recover from interruption by
idempotent resume, reversible drafts and durable job state.

Status discipline: Unknown, Estimated, Source-confirmed, Reconciled, Reviewed, Filed, Paid and

7. Automation acceptance and measurement

The 99.9% no-touch target remains a goal, not a release assertion. Record denominator, issuer, format,
field, period, confidence, human correction, exception and any delayed match. Keep separate measures
for amount, date, currency, entity, paying account, category, tax, duplicate detection, card match, bank
reconciliation and posting. Financially consequential fields require explicit control checks, not just a
general AI confidence score.

The existing 200-row issuer sample and 99.5% amount/date / 97% category thresholds are eligibility
screening minima, not statistical proof of a 0.1% end-to-end error rate. Use independent holdout
documents across formats and time, publish confidence intervals and failure examples, require zero
unexplained financial discrepancies in the acceptance set, and step down automation when corrections
or drift exceed the approved policy. Never use a test dataset to train the rule being tested. An accuracy
dashboard must display numerator, denominator and date.

11. Legal and regulatory validation contract

The relevant Income-tax Act, 2025 commences on 1 April 2026; historic periods require their applicable
rules. This does not by itself certify any generated tax schedule. For every entity, assessment/tax year
and filing type, version the rule source, official form/schema, effective date, applicability, amendment
history, validation result, professional reviewer, submission and acknowledgement, and later payment
proof. Do not adopt a secondary article as the authoritative format. Repeat the same process for GST,
TDS/TCS, trust and 80G records, Companies Act/MCA, payroll statutory data, Malaysia-specific reporting
only when the entity is enabled, and DPDP privacy obligations according to their phased
commencement.
The term “regulatory-ready” is earned only when the specific official form/schema and a qualified
reviewer confirm its applicability. Any rule change that alters money, filing content or classification
requires explicit approval and regression tests. A harmless government due-date extension may follow
V3 Decision 16 only after authoritative-source authentication, effective-date check, audit record and
reversibility.

12. Permissions, privacy, security and recovery

Roles from V3 are owner, adult family member, delegate, bookkeeper, accountant, auditor, company
secretary, authorised reviewer and administrator. Enforce scoped permissions on the server and
database for view, upload, draft edit, propose, post, approve, export, share, delete unposted, manage
access and reopen. Professional verification is not delegated to a cosmetic label. Adult family members
opt into shared visibility. Cross-entity testing attempts direct record IDs, exports, search, background
jobs and share links.

Google sign-in, passkeys, biometric-backed platform authenticators and a local four-digit quick unlock
must not become a weaker path to privileged actions. The PIN is device-bound, rate-limited, revocable,
not a substitute for server authentication, and never permits consequential approvals or sensitive
exports. Timeouts: 5 minutes phone, 15 minutes desktop, with step-up for privileged activity. Verify
session expiry and revocation across tabs and downloaded report links.

RPO of 15 minutes and RTO of four hours are targets conditional on purchased capabilities and
demonstrated restores, not claims. Test independent database and object-storage copies, point-in-time
recovery, checksum validation, encryption keys, identity and permission restoration, reconciled totals,
job resume and recovery of original evidence. Quarterly isolated restore drills and monthly full owner
exports remain mandatory. Security minimums belong to Phase 0—not postponed to Phase 5.
